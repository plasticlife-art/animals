Animal sprites: "Free Top-Down Hunt Animals Pixel Sprite Pack" by CraftPix.
Source: https://craftpix.net/freebies/free-top-down-hunt-animals-pixel-sprite-pack/
License: https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/

What that license allows and forbids, in short:

  - Commercial use in this game is allowed. No attribution is required, though
    the authors appreciate credit.
  - Redistributing the art source files is NOT allowed. That includes shipping
    them in a form another person could lift the assets out of.

Two more CraftPix freebies are used the same way, under the same license:
"Free Pixel Art Plants for Farm" for assets/tiles/props_atlas.png, and
"Free Basic Pixel Art UI for RPG" for the UI skin under assets/ui/.

Source files are not in the repository.

  The raw PNG/PSD/ASEPRITE files of all three packs live only on a developer's
  disk, in the assets/craftpix*/ folders. Each folder's SOURCE.md names the pack
  to download. .gitignore keeps the files out of version control, and .gdignore
  keeps Godot from importing them into, or exporting them with, the game. Only
  the atlases composited from them are tracked.

  Commits made before this change still contain the source files. Before making
  the repository public, rewrite that history or publish a fresh repository
  without it.

Terrain tiles, shadow blob and the placeholder animal atlases are generated by
tools/generate_placeholder_atlases.py and carry no third-party license.
The per-species atlases under assets/sprites/ are composited from the CraftPix
pack by tools/build_pack_atlases.py and inherit the license above.
