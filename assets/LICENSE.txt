Source: https://craftpix.net/freebies/free-top-down-hunt-animals-pixel-sprite-pack/
License: https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/

What that license allows and forbids, in short:

  - Commercial use in this game is allowed. No attribution is required, though
    the authors appreciate credit.
  - Redistributing the art source files is NOT allowed. That includes shipping
    them in a form another person could lift the assets out of.

WARNING, read before making this repository public.

  The raw PNG/PSD/ASEPRITE files under assets/craftpix/ are committed here
  because the repository is private. Publishing the repository would make those
  files downloadable by anyone, which the license forbids. Before flipping the
  repository to public, move assets/craftpix/ out of version control (gitignore
  it plus a fetch script, or a private asset store) and keep only the composited
  atlases if their license permits, or regenerate placeholders.

Terrain tiles, shadow blob and the placeholder animal atlases are generated by
tools/generate_placeholder_atlases.py and carry no third-party license.
The per-species atlases under assets/sprites/ are composited from the CraftPix
pack by tools/build_pack_atlases.py and inherit the license above.
